Restore Pro

Open Breathe Now, tap the settings button, then tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that bought Pro. Breathe Now Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

Sounds or haptics aren’t playing

Open settings and check that phase sounds or haptics are enabled. iPhone sound settings and system haptic settings can also affect what you hear or feel.

The breathing pace feels uncomfortable

Stop the session and return to normal breathing. Choose a gentler pattern or don’t use the app. Breathe Now is a breathing guide, not a medical device, and it does not diagnose or treat any condition.
