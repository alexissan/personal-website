A monograph in ten plates

ESCAPE­MENT

Ten watches, and the century they measured.

Edition of one · 01/01 Ten plates · 1904–1989 Drawn & set in the browser Alexis Santos

The escapement is the part that refuses. Every second, it stops the train of wheels dead, lets exactly one beat through, and locks again. All the elegance of a mechanical watch is downstream of that small, stubborn act of holding back. What follows is ten of them — each dial below is drawn, not photographed, and each is running now, at its own true beat.
